## 2. Draft package
Post 1: visit and hours. Post 2: bread and pastries. Post 3: counter collection.
Each: 40-60 words, one next step, no unsupported claim.
FAQ: hours / range / collection / allergen information.
Allergen answer: TO CONFIRM with owner before publication. Never guess safety information.

Prompt: "Use only this fictional brief. Draft the agreed posts and FAQ. Mark missing facts TO CONFIRM.
Do not invent prices, health claims, testimonials or sales: [brief]."

## 3. Fact and asset ledger
Sentence | brief evidence | keep/correct/remove | reviewer.
- "Open Sunday": REMOVE (brief says Tuesday-Saturday).
- "Delivery available": REMOVE (no evidence).
- "Bread and pastries": KEEP (supported).

Asset | creator/license | permitted use | alt text.
Use original text-only artwork if you have no permissioned images.
Check contrast, readable text and captions. Do not use another person's likeness/voice without permission.

## 4. Controlled revisions
Keep v1. Practice request: hours first, shorter collection copy.
Create v2, retain 40-60 words per post and recheck facts.
Unknown price request stays pending.
Version | requested change | evidence affected | action | approval.

## 5. Scope and costs
Included: three posts, four FAQ answers, one revision round.
Excluded: automatic posting, ad spend, sales guarantees, unsupported claims.
Illustrative internal labour: (1h brief + 2h drafts + 1h checks + 1h revision/handover) x EUR 20 = EUR 100.
This is not a market rate, proposed price or profit. Expenses, tax and overhead remain to be checked.
Use the proposal builder to choose fees separately and agree deliverables, approvals and payment terms.
