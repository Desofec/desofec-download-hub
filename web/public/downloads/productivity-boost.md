# AI Productivity Boost

By Desofec AI Studio. Use AI to write, plan and research faster.

## Daily routine (10 minutes)
1. **Morning**: "Here are my tasks: [LIST]. Group them, estimate time, and propose a schedule for [HOURS] hours."
2. **Midday**: "Summarize these notes into decisions and next steps: [NOTES]"
3. **Evening**: "Based on what I did today: [LIST], draft tomorrow's top 3 priorities."

## Writing
- Draft: "Write a [TYPE] about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE] in a [TONE] tone, under [N] words."
- Edit: "Tighten this text, keep my voice, flag anything unclear: [TEXT]"
- Emails: "Draft a polite reply that says [POINT]. Keep it to 4 sentences."

## Planning
- "Break [GOAL] into milestones with a weekly checklist."
- "List risks for this plan and how to reduce each: [PLAN]"

## Research
- "Give me an overview of [TOPIC] and 5 questions I should ask next."
- "Compare [A] and [B] in a table by cost, ease and best use case."
- Always confirm important claims with a trusted source.

## Habits that keep it useful
- Give context and examples.
- Keep a personal prompt library of what works.
- Review everything before sending it.
- Keep private or confidential information out of prompts.
