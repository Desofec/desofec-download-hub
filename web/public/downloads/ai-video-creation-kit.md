# AI Video Creation Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Create a responsible 30-second video with your own or permissioned assets.

## Brief
Audience | one message | platform/aspect ratio | next step | verified facts | owned assets | budget limit.

## Storyboard
- 0-10 seconds: problem | visual | narration | on-screen text.
- 10-20 seconds: demonstration | visual | narration | on-screen text.
- 20-30 seconds: next step | visual | narration | on-screen text.

Read the script aloud to check timing.

## Asset permission log
Asset | source | owner | licence/permission | allowed use | attribution | AI-generated?
Get permission for faces and voices. Do not use unlicensed music, private footage or deceptive impersonation. AI output is not documentary evidence.

## Production
Film your own clips or test one optional generated clip. Review distortions and claims.
Assemble in an editor such as CapCut or Descript, trim, add approved text and permissioned sound.

## Accessibility and final review
Correct captions, names, timing and meaningful sound descriptions.
Watch on a phone with sound off: check readable text, contrast and caption placement.
Avoid flashing effects. Prepare a transcript where supported.
View the exported file and check current destination format and AI disclosure rules.
Confirm credits, accurate title/description and one feedback date.
