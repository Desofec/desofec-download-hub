# Multilingual Work and Translation Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Practise a careful English-to-Spanish translation with a fictional notice. AI is optional; a fluent draft, back-translation or checklist is not proof of accuracy or certification.

## 1. Fictional source and brief

Harbor Learning Centre is fictional. Audience: general readers. Purpose: understand a workshop notice. There is no real recipient.

```text
M1: Harbor Learning Centre offers a free digital skills workshop.
M2: It takes place on 2030-06-14 from 14:00 to 15:00 UTC in Room B.
M3: Registration closes on 2030-06-12; there are 20 seats.
M4: Materials are provided.
```

For real work, record source language, target language, intended audience, region, purpose, tone and accessible format. Ask when a preference is unknown; do not assume one Spanish variety suits every audience.

## 2. Permission and privacy

- Use the fictional notice for this exercise. Before translating real material, check its rights and obtain any required permission.
- Remove unnecessary names, contacts and confidential or sensitive details before prompting. Keep originals in an approved place.
- Check the current availability, charges, data handling and organisational rules for any external tool. Do not assume a free tier or private processing.
- If the text cannot be shared with a service, use an approved alternative or work offline. No paid tool is required for this exercise.

## 3. Glossary and protected facts

| English | Spanish | Meaning to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| free | gratuito | no charge |
| registration | inscripción | registration, not attendance |
| seats | plazas | capacity, not confirmed attendees |
| Harbor Learning Centre | Harbor Learning Centre | keep the name |
| UTC | UTC | keep the time zone |

Protected facts: **2030-06-14**, **14:00-15:00 UTC**, **Room B**, **2030-06-12**, **20 seats**, free workshop, materials provided. Record uncertain terms or regional preferences for a competent reviewer instead of guessing.

## 4. Draft, compare and correct

Translate by source ID and compare each segment with the English source.

```text
M1: Harbor Learning Centre ofrece un taller gratuito de competencias digitales.
M2: Se celebra el 2030-06-14 de 14:00 a 15:00 UTC en la sala B.
M3: La inscripción se cierra el 2030-06-12; hay 20 plazas.
M4: Se proporcionan los materiales.
```

Practice error check: a candidate that says **15:00-16:00 UTC** in M2 or **30 plazas** in M3 is wrong. Restore **14:00-15:00 UTC** and **20 plazas**. Check free, the registration date, room and materials too; do not invent a deadline time or silently convert the time zone. Back-translation can help identify issues but cannot establish correctness.

Optional prompt, using only approved fictional text: “Translate M1-M4 into Spanish for general readers. Return each source ID, preserve all facts and UTC, and flag ambiguity without adding details: [M1-M4].”

## 5. Human review and private handover

- Keep the source, draft, glossary and correction notes together. A competent speaker should check meaning, tone, accessibility and suitability for the actual audience.
- For legal, medical or other high-stakes material, use an appropriately qualified professional and follow local rules. Do not claim certification or guaranteed accuracy.
- This fictional exercise has no real reviewer or recipient: approval is **BLOCKED**, feedback is **NOT COLLECTED**, and the draft stays private. Do not send it to recipients or publish it.
