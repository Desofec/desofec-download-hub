# AI Content Creator Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Plan, create and polish posts, images, voice and short videos with AI helpers.

## The 4-step creator loop
1. **Idea**: pick one audience and one message.
2. **Draft**: let AI give you a first version.
3. **Polish**: edit it in your own voice and check facts.
4. **Share**: publish only after review and with the necessary permission; record what actually happened.

## Prompt templates
- **Ideas**: "Give me 10 post ideas about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Mark the 3 easiest to make this week."
- **Script**: "Write a 45-second video script about [TOPIC]: a hook in the first 5 seconds, 3 key points, one call to action."
- **Captions**: "Write 3 caption options under [N] words for this post: [POST]. Friendly tone."
- **Image brief**: "Describe an image for [PURPOSE]: subject, style, colors, mood and what to avoid."
- **Repurpose**: "Turn this article into a short post, a 5-point list and a one-line headline: [TEXT]"

The 45-second template is for general use. The content-creation tutorial's optional
storyboard is 30 seconds in three 10-second scenes; it is a plan, not a finished video.

## Simple workflows
- Outline in a chat assistant, build slides with a presentation tool, add polish in a design tool.
- Record a voice note, let a transcript tool clean it, then edit the text into a post.
- Make a short video from your script with a video editor, then add captions.

## Responsible creating
- Do not copy other people's work or imitate a real person's voice without permission.
- Say when AI helped, if your audience or platform expects it.
- Check facts, names, numbers and licences before you publish.
- Keep private information out of every prompt.
- Keep the fictional Cedar Bakery exercise private. There is no real owner to approve publication and no result to report.

## Your first 30 minutes
1. Choose one topic you know well.
2. Generate 10 ideas and pick one.
3. Draft, edit it in your voice, and publish with permission or save it privately.
