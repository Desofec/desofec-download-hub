# Freelance Client Discovery & Project Scoping Kit

Fictional practice only. This is not a contract, legal advice, client approval or a
promise of customers, employment, income or savings. No paid tool or AI is required.
Lessons and this worksheet are English; the lab interface supports French, German and Spanish.

## 1. Start with questions, not a promise

Opening request: "Can you automate our spreadsheet and save us lots of time?"
This does not define deliverables, access permission, price, deadline or evidence of savings.
Use the public discovery lab or write in your own notes. The simulator has fixed answers,
not an AI conversation, a real interview or evidence of market demand.

Ask neutrally:
- What happens today, what fails, and what source evidence exists?
- What should the first delivery include, and what is explicitly excluded?
- Who may approve access, retention and deletion?
- Who compares the output with the source, and when are they available?

Known fictional facts: an owner compares five sample orders manually. The sample delivery
is one checked CSV, an exception list and a short handover. There is no measured baseline
or demand evidence. Real access permission, budget, deadline and reviewer availability
remain unknown. One correction round would need agreement.

For a real discussion, obtain permission and use non-identifying role-level notes only.

## 5. Save and hand off responsibly

Open an existing service project, then Open client discovery and scoping.
Reload saved project and discovery before editing. Save explicitly on the device.
This shared preparation path is not a separate service project type.
The saved report includes discovery, fit choices, evidence notes and the proposed change;
it excludes proposals, costs, results, workbook text and review copies.
Inspect all included text before downloading or manually sharing.

In the connected proposal builder, reload its draft and choose Start from saved discovery.
Confirm replacement. This copies saved scope fields only, leaves fees and internal costs
blank and clears generated text. Unknowns, fit decisions and changes stay in discovery.
Regenerate, inspect and explicitly save the proposal; nothing is sent to a client.

Device and account copies are separate. Account upload requires consent in the workspace.
Delete saved discovery, account snapshots and downloaded files separately. Removing discovery
does not remove text previously copied into a proposal. Old exports may retain earlier text.

## 6. Check your practice

- Unknown and No each keep fit paused.
- All Yes without evidence keeps fit paused.
- Missing scope fields keep fit paused.
- A proposed change leaves original scope unchanged.
- Reload loses public-lab edits but preserves explicitly saved private drafts.
- Proposal import leaves money blank and never claims agreement.
- No live outreach, credentials, uploads or AI calls occur.

Optional AI prompt: "Review this fictional discovery note for unsupported assumptions,
unclear scope and missing permissions. Ask neutral questions. Do not invent answers,
budgets, deadlines, approvals, legal terms or savings." Verify any suggestions yourself.
