# AI Small Business Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Complete the AI for Small Business tutorial to create a reviewed content pack.

## Offer brief
Offer, audience, problem solved, verified features/price/delivery terms, evidence sources and dates, assumptions, claims you must not make.

## Customer research
Ask: what task is difficult, what have you tried, and what would help?
Collect only needed information with permission. Remove identifying details before using AI.
Summarise real responses as: theme | anonymised evidence | response count | uncertainty.
No responses yet? Record research to do; never invent findings.

## Product description prompt
"Use only this brief to draft 100 words: audience, features, limitations and one next step. Do not invent prices, testimonials, guarantees or benefits. Mark missing facts NEEDS REVIEW: [brief]."

## FAQ worksheet
Question | answer | actual policy/source | checked date.
Unknown answers go to your verified human support route. Do not invent policies or legal advice.

## One-week campaign
Goal | channel | message | next step.
Day | draft | claim evidence | asset permission | approver.
Draft three posts, review them yourself and only email recipients who consented.

## Results
Date | chosen metric | result | time spent | uncertainty | one improvement.
Do not promise sales or infer causation from a small campaign.
