# AI for Teachers and Students

By Desofec AI Studio. Use AI to support teaching and learning while protecting privacy and doing your own thinking.

## For teachers
- Draft a lesson outline from a learning objective, age/level and duration.
- Generate practice questions with a separate answer key.
- Ask for alternative explanations and accessibility checks.
- Review facts, bias, reading level and curriculum fit before sharing.

## For students
- Ask for a patient tutor that gives hints before answers.
- Practise one question at a time and explain your reasoning.
- Ask for feedback on your own draft; do not ask AI to do an assignment for you.
- Verify important facts with class materials and follow course rules.

## A safe prompt
“I am a [teacher/student] working on [subject and level]. Help me [task] without asking for personal student information. Show what I should verify, and keep the learner responsible for their own work.”

Never upload student names, grades, disability details, private work or other identifying information to a public AI service. Follow your institution’s privacy, age and disclosure rules.
