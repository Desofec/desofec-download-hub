# AI Career Skills Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Prepare a truthful application, not a guaranteed job outcome.

## Role evidence
Role | verified employer source/date.
Requirement | real example | gap to learn.
Remove personal identifiers and confidential employer details before sharing with an AI service.

## Resume prompt
"Rewrite these verified notes as concise bullets. Use only actions, tools and outcomes I supplied. Do not invent metrics, qualifications, dates or employers. Mark missing evidence for review: [notes]."

## Cover letter
Correct role | verified employer detail | why interested | two requirements with real examples | short closing.
Read it aloud; check every claim. Add contact details only to your private final document.

## Three interview stories
Situation | task | your action | actual result | lesson learned.
Practise follow-ups out loud in your own words. No fabricated stories or confidential client details.

## Private application tracker
Employer | role | verified source | applied date | status | next action | follow-up date.
Check recipient and attachments yourself before sending. Verify employers and beware requests for payment or sensitive documents from unverified contacts.

## Two-week learning plan
One recurring skill gap | small project | first resource | three sessions | evidence of learning | review date.
Follow employer AI-use rules, including disclosure where required.
