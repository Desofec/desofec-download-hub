# AI Research Prompt Kit

By Desofec AI Studio. Use AI to organise research, not to replace source checking.

## Research loop
1. Narrow the topic to one answerable question.
2. Find real sources and open the originals.
3. Build an evidence table: claim, passage, source/date, limitation.
4. Summarise themes and disagreements without filling gaps.
5. Check every citation and disclose AI help when required.

## Prompts
- **Narrow the question:** “Suggest three focused, neutral research questions about [topic] for [purpose]. For each, say what evidence would answer it.”
- **Find sources:** “Suggest search phrases and reliable source types for [question]. Do not invent citations; mark details I must verify.”
- **Compare sources:** “Using only these sources [sources], list what they agree on, disagree on and do not address. Cite exact passages.”
- **Draft carefully:** “Write a short synthesis from this verified evidence table [table]. Separate facts, interpretations and open questions. Add no new claims.”
- **Verify:** “Check each sentence against these original sources [sources]. Flag unsupported, outdated or incorrectly attributed claims.”

AI may fabricate citations or misread a source. Confirm every source exists and supports the claim. Follow your school or workplace rules.
