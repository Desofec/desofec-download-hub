# Beginner AI Starter Pack

By Desofec AI Studio. Copy, paste, and adapt. Replace [BRACKETS] with your details.

## How to prompt well
1. **Role**: tell the AI who it should act as.
2. **Task**: say exactly what you want.
3. **Context**: give background, audience and constraints.
4. **Format**: ask for a list, table, email, etc.
5. **Iterate**: reply with "shorter", "simpler", or "give 3 more options".

## Prompt templates
- **Explain it simply**: "Explain [TOPIC] as if I'm a beginner. Use one analogy and 3 bullet points."
- **Summarize**: "Summarize the text below in 5 bullets, then list 2 open questions: [TEXT]"
- **Brainstorm**: "Give me 10 ideas for [GOAL] for [AUDIENCE]. Rank the top 3 and say why."
- **Rewrite**: "Rewrite this to be clearer and friendlier, under [N] words: [TEXT]"
- **Plan**: "Create a 7-day plan to learn [SKILL] with 30 minutes per day."
- **Critique**: "Act as a strict reviewer. List the 5 biggest weaknesses in [DRAFT]."

## Quick workflow ideas
- Turn meeting notes into action items with owners.
- Draft replies to common emails, then edit them yourself.
- Convert a long article into a study sheet with quiz questions.

## Safety checklist
- Do not paste passwords, private customer data, or confidential work.
- Verify facts, numbers and links before you use them.
- You are responsible for the final result.

## Your first 20 minutes
1. Pick one small task you do weekly.
2. Try two of the templates above on it.
3. Save the prompt that worked best.
