## 2. Original lesson plan

Create an original readable handout and text-only alternative.
Do not copy paid courses, licensed tests or other creators' materials without permission.

## 3. Checked key and 0-4 practice rubric
1 point: identify 10:00 as supported.
1 point: identify "60 minutes" as contradicted and correct to 30.
1 point: flag "certificate for everyone" as unknown.
1 point: propose asking organiser/checking verified policy rather than guessing.
This score is not a qualification or evidence of wider ability.
Blank answers mean missing evidence, not proof of inability.
